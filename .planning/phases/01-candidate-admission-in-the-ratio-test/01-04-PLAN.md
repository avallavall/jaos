<objective>
Take the measurement that decides this phase: a same-instance time ratio at
`J=1` between the candidate and its parent, over the standard set, reported as
a geometric mean of per-instance ratios with `truss` named separately, and read
against the 4.2% threshold.

Purpose: work units cannot answer this question. The counter is blind to memory
layout and cache behaviour by construction, and locality is exactly where
trading a dense sequential walk for a sparser one is won or lost. Plan `01-03`
has already established the two things the other two legs of D45 can say —
digests for correctness, work units for determinism. This is the third leg, and
it is the one that says whether the change bought anything.

Output: a per-instance timing table, a geometric mean, `truss` on its own line,
a callgrind re-read of `admit_candidate`'s instruction share, and a verdict of
ACCEPT, REJECT or INCONCLUSIVE.

**This plan is expensive in wall-clock time.** The standard set is about eight
minutes at `J=1`, and the protocol needs two binaries alternated over at least
three rounds — roughly fifty minutes of machine time before the first number is
readable, plus the callgrind run on top. It is worth stating the cost before
launching rather than after. Nothing here can be run at `J=12`: a parallel
run's seconds are inflated by an amount nobody measured, and the runner says so
on every line of its output.
</objective>

<threshold_note>

D-13 fixes the threshold at **4.2%, derived as three times the harness's
measured repeatability of 1.4%**. That is the locked decision and it is what
this plan measures against.

One thing to carry into D93 rather than quietly resolve here: the `jaos-measure`
skill states the harness repeats itself to **1.3% (D60)**, not 1.4%. Three times
1.3% is 3.9%. The two figures are close enough that no plausible result lands
between them by accident, but a threshold in this project is a number with a
measurement on both sides of it, and D93 must say which repeatability figure the
derivation used and why the other exists. Record both figures in the SUMMARY;
do not pick one silently, and do not move the threshold — 4.2% is what D-13
locked and this plan measures against it.

</threshold_note>
