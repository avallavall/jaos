<objective>
The scaffolding, proved end to end, while almost nothing is removed. One
reduction — columns already fixed as loaded — a reduced model built alongside
the caller's, a postsolve arena replayed LIFO, and the build switch that
compiles the whole thing out.

Purpose: this is D-01, and its rationale is the whole shape of the phase. The
correctness machinery is the part that can be wrong in a way nothing catches — a
postsolve that maps an index wrongly produces a *plausible* answer in the right
shape, not a crash. Proving that machinery while the model is nearly unchanged
is the only moment it can be proved cheaply. Every reduction after this one
arrives with its own postsolve record and its own measured contribution, so the
postsolve risk is paid in instalments rather than in one diff.

This plan is the tracer: one path through every layer the phase will touch —
`jm_dual_simplex` → `jm_presolve_run` → `sx_init` → `run` → `publish` →
`jm_postsolve_expand` → the caller's `sol_*` arrays → `jaos_check_solution` —
wired end to end and verified end to end. It is production code, not a
prototype: everything it lands stays.

Output: `src/presolve.c` as its own module with its types and prototypes in
`src/jaos_internal.h` — that is D-05: `src/simplex.c` is already 3,829 lines,
and this tree has no per-module header, so a new `src/*.c` cannot create an
include cycle. Also the
two call sites in `src/simplex.c`, the per-family counters in the record, and a
new `tests/test_presolve.c` in which the negative case is written before the
positive one is believed.
</objective>

    The reduction this plan ships is fixed columns: a column whose
    `col_lower[j]` equals `col_upper[j]` exactly as loaded. It is dropped from
    the reduced model, its contribution `a_ij * value` is subtracted from both
    finite bounds of every row it appears in, its cost times its value goes into
    the reduced objective offset, and one `JM_PS_FIXED_COL` record is pushed.

    fixed only by being loaded that way. Scan columns in ascending `j`; state in

    status is `JAOS_BASIS_AT_LOWER`, which the checker's rule permits because a
    fixed variable carries no sign condition at all; and the reduced cost is the
    column's own cost minus the dot product of its original column against the
    postsolved row duals. Derive that against `sign_condition` as read, not from

    diff exists because of it.

    publishes a complete answer on its own. The objective is the reduced offset;
    every row's activity is computed from the caller's original coefficients and
    the recovered column values; every dual is zero and every row status is
    whichever bound the activity rests on, or basic when it rests on neither.

## Trust Boundaries

JAOS is an embedded numerical library. It opens no socket, spawns no process
and has no authentication surface of its own. Two boundaries exist and only the
first is new work for this phase.

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| caller → `jaos_model` → `src/presolve.c` | The caller supplies `num_row`, `num_col`, `num_nz`, the CSC index array and the bound arrays, directly or through a parsed MPS or LP file. This phase adds a new consumer of all of it: presolve reads those dimensions, derives allocation sizes from them, and writes indices derived from them into the postsolve arena. |
