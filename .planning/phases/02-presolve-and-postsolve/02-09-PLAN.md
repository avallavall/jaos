<objective>
Writing it down, in the four documents that take four different parts of it.

Purpose: the reasoning about a closed decision goes to `DECISIONS.md`; what is
still open goes to `.planning/`; a feature's existence or absence goes to
`SPECS.md`; and the changelog is a changelog, not a decision record. Two entries
are owed here rather than one, because the work-unit billing is its own one-way
decision and folding it into the presolve entry would bury the fact that every
historical work figure changed meaning.

There is a second job. The roadmap and `STATE.md` both record an open question
— how the comparison ladder is recalibrated once JAOS has a presolve — and the
answer is already on disk in `bench/compare/README.md`, which says presolve
moves into the base rung for everyone and a new rung appears above, and that a
record carries the tier definition it was taken under because a comparison
against a differently-defined rung is not a comparison. That is a stale
cross-reference to correct, not a question to answer, and correcting it here
saves phase 5 from re-deriving it. The `make compare` re-run stays phase 5's
work: a number taken under WSL is a development number and cannot close a gate.

Output: two decision entries, a changelog entry, a `SPECS.md` that says presolve
exists without repairing figures it did not re-measure, and a roadmap and state
file that agree with the tree.
</objective>
