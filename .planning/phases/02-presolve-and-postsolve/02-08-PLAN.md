<objective>
What presolve is worth on this solver, on this set, measured for JAOS rather
than carried over from a reading of two competitors — with the negative control
that makes the number interpretable.

Purpose: roadmap criterion 3 and D-15. Two ratios, reported separately, because
presolve trades setup work for iteration work and one number hides which way the
trade went. A geometric mean of per-instance ratios, never a ratio of totals,
because two instances are 74% of the standard set's total. At `J=1`, because a
parallel run's seconds are inflated and the runner says so on every line.

And a negative control that is not optional. Phase 1 is the receipt: eight
instances whose work was bit-identical under both binaries — instances the
change provably could not speed up — read 0.9699x paired and 0.9356x pooled,
a 3.0 to 6.4% "improvement" where the truth is zero, against a 2.91% headline.
The control was sitting unused in the committed record and was found by an
independent audit after the phase's gates had passed. Here it is a task, and the
instances that form it were already named by `02-07`.

**No target exists and none is invented.** `REQ-presolve`'s acceptance is
recorded as absent deliberately. D81's 1.417x and 1.136x are readings of HiGHS
and SoPlex. This plan produces a number and states what it is; it does not
produce a verdict against a bar, because there is no bar and inventing one would
contradict the requirement it claims to serve.

Output: two ratios, a control beside them, the raw readings committed, and
`jaos-measurer`'s judgement of all of it.
</objective>
